(01) About HINSON Tiles

HINSON Ceramics is a tile studio for tactile, architectural surfaces. We work with clay, quiet glazes and small variations that make a wall feel unmistakably yours.

HINSON is a ceramic tile studio for spaces that feel quiet, tactile and made by hand.

(02) Materials

The beauty of a tiled surface

Our tiles begin with clay and stay close to it: mineral bodies, ash, sand and glazes that shift gently across a wall, floor or backsplash.

01 / Wall tile

Clay, set in place.

Small-format tiles with a softly irregular edge, made to build rhythm across a quiet surface.

02 / Kiln mark

Fire makes the mark.

Each firing shifts the colour and depth. Variation is not a flaw; it is the signature of the kiln.

03 / Surface study

Quiet colour, deep surface.

Layered glazes catch the light without shouting, bringing depth to kitchens, baths and thresholds.

Hinson / The studio

A slower practice designed around clay, fire and the tile

We keep the studio deliberately small. Each tile is pressed, edged, glazed and fired in a process that leaves room for touch, chance and the character of the material.

(05) The ritual

Made for the room

Tiles become architectural through repetition. A measured grid, a quiet joint, a surface that changes with the light and stays with the room for years.

01 / Hold

Feel the weight.

Balanced forms and softened edges make each object part of the gesture, not just the setting.

02 / Use

Let it gather a life.

Small variations, changing light and the first marks of use are part of the design.

03 / Keep

Choose what stays.

Built for repeat mornings, shared meals and the quiet pleasure of returning to the same piece.
